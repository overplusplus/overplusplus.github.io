Additional Results

We demonstrate compositing of foreground (FG) and background (BG) layers sourced from different domains. When FG and BG originate from disparate sources, the naive composite lacks consistent environmental interaction effects. We apply $\textit{Over++}$ (without a mask) to synthesize in-context interaction effects and produce a coherent, visually consistent composited video.

We duplicate the foreground video, followed by resizing and relocating the foreground. $\textit{Over++}$ is able to generate effects for both the duplicated foreground and the original foreground.
