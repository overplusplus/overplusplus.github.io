Failure Cases

With a large classifier-free guidance (CFG) scale, $\textit{Over++}$ may alter the overall color tone, leading to over-saturated content \cite{sadat2024eliminating}. In addition, $\textit{Over++}$ can sometimes hallucinate environmental effects that are not present in the original background with large CFG scale.
