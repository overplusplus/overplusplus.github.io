Over++: Generative Video Compositing for Layer Interaction Effects
Supplementary Material

Video compositing in real production environments requires artists to reason about foreground–background separation and the complex interaction effects between them (e.g., shadows, reflections, dust; example courtesy of ILM).


We introduce $\textbf{augmented compositing}$, a new generative task that aims to synthesize realistic interaction effects while preserving both the foreground subject and the background scene. This task extends conventional compositing by explicitly modeling semi-transparent, spatially varying environmental effects.


Our method, $\textit{Over++}$, is the first to address the augmented compositing task. Despite the limited availability of training data \cite{gillman2025force}, $\textit{Over++}$ exhibits strong generalization across a wide range of visual effects, as demonstrated in the benchmarking results below. In addition, we show that $\textit{Over++}$ enables several downstream applications—including text-guided and mask-guided editing—highlighting its flexibility and broader usefulness. Further details are provided in the sections that follow.
