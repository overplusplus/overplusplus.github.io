Over++: Generative Video Compositing for Layer Interaction Effects
Supplementary Material

Video compositing in real production environments requires reasoning about foreground–background separation and complex interaction effects such as shadows, reflections, and airborne particles. The accompanying examples (from professional VFX studio demonstrations) highlight the workflow and challenges involved.

We introduce $\textbf{augmented compositing}$, a generative task that synthesizes realistic interaction effects while preserving both the foreground subject and the background scene. This task extends conventional compositing without explicitly rendering the interaction effects.

Our method, $\textit{Over++}$, is the first to address the augmented compositing task. Despite the limited training data (around 1K videos versus 15K in Force Prompting \cite{gillman2025force}), $\textit{Over++}$ demonstrates strong generalization across a wide range of visual effects. Additionally, $\textit{Over++}$ enables several downstream applications, including text-guided and mask-guided editing, highlighting its flexibility and broader usefulness. Further details are provided in the sections that follow.
